A Guide for Parents

Let your teen use AI to turn their own ideas into something they can show

An introductory AI coding course designed for high-school students. No programming background needed — your child learns to build their own web pages, tools, and small games simply by describing what they want to an AI.

🎯 No coding background needed 🧩 10 lessons · one per week 👤 1-on-1 private lessons 🚀 Finish a real, shareable project
What this course teaches

Not "memorizing code" — "making ideas real"

Just describe it, and build

Vibe coding is a new way to build: your child tells an AI what they want in plain language, and the AI helps turn it into something that runs. The focus shifts from "can you write code" to "can you describe your idea clearly."

The skills last longer than any tool

Along the way, your child practises breaking a fuzzy idea into steps, trying things out, fixing mistakes, and improving until it's done. These problem-solving habits outlast any single tool or app.

In one sentence: by the end of the course, your child will have built a small project of their own, from scratch — one they can open and share with others — starting from nothing more than "I have an idea."

How I teach

Learning by doing: every student has their own project

I use Project-Based Learning in a private 1-on-1 format. At the start of the course, I work with your child to set a small goal they genuinely want to build, and to work on it in their own time. Each session is for introducing new ideas, live demonstrations, and helping your child get unstuck — paced entirely around them.

01

Pick your own small goal

For example: a web page to help you revise vocabulary, a sign-up form for a club, or a simple game. The goal belongs to the student — motivation comes from "I want to make this."

02

Build it in your own time

Apply what you learn in class to your own project. The real learning happens in the doing — getting stuck, and finding a way through.

03

Dedicated help every session

Because it's 1-on-1, every session is shaped around your child. We spend time wherever they're stuck and move at exactly their pace — no waiting, no falling behind.

The learning journey

Ten weeks, from first taste to a public showcase

The course runs in four phases, step by step. The weeks marked in amber are the key moments when your child's own project moves forward.

PHASE 1

Getting started

W01

Make something with AI for the first time

Build a working little web page or game within minutes, and feel first-hand how "just describe it" works. Start thinking: what do I want to make?

▹ Start planning your project
W02

Meet your AI coding partner

Learn to use Claude Code — describing tasks in plain language and watching it build, step by step, while you stay in control.

PHASE 2

From idea to prototype

W03

Find a good idea

Learn how to judge whether an idea is worth building and how to describe it clearly. This week each student locks in their project topic.

▹ Project topic locked in
W04

Build your first screen

Turn your idea into a visible single-page prototype — give your project a face first.

W05

Add pages and interaction

Make it clickable, switch between pages, and respond to actions — it starts to feel like a real product.

PHASE 3

Make it smarter

W06

Give your project AI powers

Add AI features — like generating text or images — into your own project.

W07

When things break

More advanced work, plus the most important lesson of all: errors are normal. How to track down a problem step by step and get the AI to help fix it.

PHASE 4

Finish and show

W08

Polish and gather feedback

Move your project from "works" to "works well," and share it with friends or family to get real feedback.

▹ Project polish
W09

Publish to the web

Learn to put your project online as a link anyone can open — actually "ship" it.

▹ Project goes live
W10

Showcase day

Every student presents their project from zero to finished — the original idea, the obstacles, and how they solved them.

▹ Public showcase

Parent FAQ

Things you might be wondering

QMy child has never coded — is that okay?
Yes — that's exactly the starting point. We assume no background and build up from "make your first thing just by describing it."
QDo we need to buy expensive equipment?
No. A regular laptop with a stable internet connection (Windows or Mac) is enough.
QI don't know how to code — can I still support my child?
You don't need any coding knowledge. The best support is simply asking now and then: "What are you making? How's it going?" — giving them a chance to explain their project to you.
QWhat is Claude Code, and do we need to pay for it?
Claude Code is the AI coding tool we use throughout the course. A paid Claude subscription is required — please set this up before the first session. I can send you setup instructions once you enrol.
QIs using AI tools safe?
The course uses mainstream, public AI coding tools. I guide students to use them correctly and responsibly, and cover basic online and data-safety habits.

About this plan: The lesson count, timing, and out-of-class hours above are suggested. Actual arrangements can flex to the class and each student's level. Every child's project moves at its own pace, and I give guidance and challenge to match.
