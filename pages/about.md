# I help people and businesses put AI to work.

Hi, I’m YY. After eight years working as an AI engineer, I’ve seen two common challenges: business owners want to adopt AI but aren’t sure where to start or how to measure its business impact; aspiring AI engineers want to break into the field but struggle to find learning that prepares them to build and ship AI products. I help both groups take the next step.

## For aspiring AI engineers

Want to move into AI engineering? I offer practical courses to help you build the skills to design, develop, and ship reliable GenAI applications. The focus is on learning by building, so you can apply what you learn to real projects.

## For business owners

Looking to become AI-native? I advise businesses on where AI can make a meaningful difference and help turn that opportunity into working systems and better ways of working. Consultancy can include identifying use cases, shaping an adoption plan, and building or improving AI workflows.
